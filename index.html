About

A little about me

I work in the development space, currently as Senior Program Lead at Mantra4Change in Bengaluru. My work sits where education, public systems, and government partnerships meet — designing programs that strengthen the systems already reaching every child, rather than building new ones beside them.

Before this I was a Mahatma Gandhi National Fellow (MGNF) with MSDE and IIM Bangalore, and I built and ran my own coaching academy in Karwar. Outside work, I love exploring new places, I'm a ginger chai over coffee person, and I'm always learning — I haven't studied tech formally, but I love trying my hand at it.

Stories

Moments from the work

01

Designing for government systems

LFE Research Symposium 2026 · "Working with the Government"

I led a session on what it really takes to design solutions inside government systems. My conviction: governments are the only actors that reach every child, so lasting change comes from strengthening what exists — not building parallel structures beside it.

02

Holding the stage at InvokED 5.0

Emcee · Conversations on leadership for a billion learners

My first time hosting an event at this scale — sharing the stage with student co-emcees and grounding two days of dialogue in the voices that matter most: students. The lesson I carried away was that leadership is a responsibility, not a position.

03

SAMARTHA & the Don Norman Design Award

Mantra4Change — DNDA25 Organization Laureate

SAMARTHA, our school-transformation program in Kalaburagi, was central to the recognition. Rooted in co-creation and shared leadership, it helped school leaders shift from compliance to genuine ownership — proof that systemic change is built with communities, not for them.

04

A week that became a community

Campus immersion · IIM Visakhapatnam

What had been names on a screen became friendships over a week of classes, simulations, and late-night badminton. I returned humbled to have made the Director's Merit List and ranked first in two terms — and reminded why I keep learning.
